Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation

TMS Therapy — A Non-Invasive Approach to Mental Health Treatment

TMS is a non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic stimulation to target areas of the brain associated with certain mental health conditions. TMS may be considered for eligible patients when clinically appropriate.

Potential Benefits

  • Non-invasive treatment
  • No surgery
  • No anesthesia
  • Outpatient treatment
  • Generally return to normal daily activities after treatment
  • Individualized to clinical needs

The TMS Treatment Process

  1. 1
    ConsultationAn initial evaluation determines whether TMS may be appropriate.
  2. 2
    Treatment PlanningThe clinical team establishes an individualized treatment protocol.
  3. 3
    Treatment SessionsSessions are performed according to the prescribed treatment plan.
  4. 4
    Progress MonitoringResponse and progress are monitored throughout treatment.
